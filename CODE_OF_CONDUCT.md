# Code of Conduct

Waffle follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People here are volunteers, users and newcomers alike.
- **Critique ideas and code, not people.** Be specific and constructive in reviews and issues.
- **No harassment of any kind:** no insults, personal attacks, sexualised language, doxxing or unwelcome attention, in public or in private.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's files or data; anonymise sample spreadsheets before sharing them.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately: use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" form on the Security tab (it reaches only the maintainers, and conduct reports are welcome there) or message a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does so, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
