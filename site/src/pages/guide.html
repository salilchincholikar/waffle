Files & tabs

Open files with ⌘O, by dragging them onto Waffle, or with Finder's Open With. Each file opens as a tab in the title bar; + opens another.

FormatOpenSave
.xlsx .xlsmYesYes — untouched parts are kept byte-for-byte
.csv .tsv .txtYes, any sizeYes — unchanged rows kept exactly
.xls .xlsb .odsYesAs a new .xlsx (the original is never modified)
  • Switch tabs with ⌃Tab / ⌃⇧Tab or ⌘⇧] / ⌘⇧[. Hover a tab to close it.
  • Window ▸ Move Tab to New Window splits a file out; Merge All Windows brings them back.
  • Big files show their first rows immediately and keep loading in the background; the status bar shows progress.
  • Sheets are the tabs along the bottom. Double-click to rename; right-click for more.

Editing

  • Just type to replace a cell; press ↩ to move down or Tab to move right. F2 or ⌃U edits the cell in place.
  • The formula row shows the cell's value or formula, the cell reference on its left, and formatting on its right: bold, colours, alignment, wrap, number formats, borders and merge.
  • Start with = for a formula. Formulas recalculate as you type.
  • Drag the small square at a selection's corner (the fill handle) to fill a series; ⌘D and ⌘R fill down and right.
  • Copy and paste works with Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. ⌘⇧V pastes values only.
  • Every action can be undone with ⌘Z.

Find & Replace

The Find field in the title bar searches every open file as you type. Press ⌘F to jump to it.

  • ↩ / ⇧↩ (or ⌘G / ⌘⇧G) step through matches, switching tabs when a match is in another file.
  • Toggles inside the field: Aa match case, ab whole cell only, ƒx look inside formulas.
  • ⌥⌘F (or the ⇄ button) opens Replace: ↩ replaces the current match, ⌥↩ replaces all, across every file.
  • Esc clears the highlights.

Clean-up tools

In the Data menu and the ✨ button in the title bar. They act on the selection (or the whole column).

  • Trim Extra Spaces, Remove Empty Rows, Remove Duplicate Rows…
  • Change Case: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case
  • Standardize Dates…: text that looks like a date becomes a real date in one format (you choose day/month order and the display format)
  • Standardize Amounts…: strips currency symbols, grouping, (negatives), CR/DR and stray spaces; understands Indian grouping (1,23,456.78)
  • Split Text into Columns…: by comma, semicolon, tab, space, pipe, dash or anything you type

Dark mode

Waffle follows your Mac's appearance. The sheet itself stays white, like paper — turn on View ▸ Dark Sheet in Dark Mode to make it dark too. Your file's colours are toned to stay readable, and nothing in the file changes.
